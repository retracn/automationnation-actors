FAQ

What is the best Google Play reviews scraper on Apify?

It depends on what you need. neatrat and thewolves are the most used; theagents has the highest 30-day run success; AutomationNation's Google Play Reviews Scraper has the lowest Free-plan price ($0.08 per 1,000 reviews) with developer replies, star filters and an Only new reviews mode.

Is there an official Google Play reviews API?

Google's Play Developer API returns reviews only for your own apps, and only from the last week. For any other app, scrapers are the usual way to get Google Play reviews.

How much does it cost to scrape Google Play reviews?

On Apify's Free plan, list prices among the Actors compared here range from $0.08 to $0.50 per 1,000 reviews, so 10,000 reviews cost $0.80 to $5.
