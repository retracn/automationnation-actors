FAQ

What is the best Google Jobs scraper on Apify?

It depends on what you value. johnvc's Google Jobs Scraper is the most used. johnvc's pay-per-result version, memo23, igview-owner and orgupdate have the highest 30-day run success (98–99%). fetch_cat has the lowest per-job price. AutomationNation's Google Jobs Scraper combines a low price ($2 per 1,000 + $0.03 per search) with an "Only new jobs" mode for daily alerts.

Is there an official Google Jobs API?

No. Google doesn't offer a public API for Google Jobs search results; Google Cloud Talent Solution only searches your own job postings. Apify Actors and third-party SERP APIs are the usual alternatives.

How much does it cost to scrape 1,000 Google Jobs listings?

On Apify's Free plan, list prices range from about $0.81 to $200 per 1,000 jobs among the Actors compared here. Most reliable options cost $2–$30 per 1,000, plus small start or per-run fees.
