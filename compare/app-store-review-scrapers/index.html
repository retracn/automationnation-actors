FAQ

What is the best App Store reviews scraper on Apify?

It depends on what you need. thewolves is the most used; theagents and johnvc have the highest 30-day run success; AutomationNation's App Store Reviews Scraper is the one that goes past 500 reviews per country with developer replies, at the lowest Free-plan price ($0.08 per 1,000 reviews).

Why are most App Store scrapers limited to 500 reviews?

They read Apple's public RSS review feed, which returns at most 10 pages of 50 reviews per country. The review API behind apps.apple.com pages much further back.

How much does it cost to scrape App Store reviews?

On Apify's Free plan, list prices among the Actors compared here range from $0.08 to $1.45 per 1,000 reviews, so 10,000 reviews cost $0.80 to $14.50 (plus any per-run fees).
