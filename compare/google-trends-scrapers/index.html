FAQ

What is the best Google Trends scraper on Apify?

It depends on what you need. apify/google-trends-scraper is the most used. data_xplorer, vnx0 and scrapesage have the highest 30-day run success. AutomationNation's Google Trends Scraper combines a low Free-plan price ($1 per 1,000 keyword reports) with full reports per keyword, unlimited-term comparisons, Trending now with news and an Only new trends mode.

Is there an official Google Trends API?

Google opened an official Google Trends API to a small group of alpha testers in 2025. For everyone else there is no public API, so scrapers and third-party SERP APIs are the usual way to get Google Trends data.

How much does it cost to scrape Google Trends?

On Apify's Free plan, list prices among the Actors compared here range from $1 to $25 per 1,000 results (one Actor charges $138 per 1,000 keyword analyses), plus small start fees on some. Paid Apify plans lower most prices.
