FAQ

Why do transcript scripts fail on servers?

YouTube asks many data-centre IPs to sign in and its web player now needs a proof-of-origin token for captions; hosted Actors handle this with app endpoints and residential IPs.

Can I get transcripts in another language?

If the video has captions in that language, yes; machine translation from YouTube is rate-limited and best effort.
