Which brands do AI assistants recommend? 115 answers from Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Claude (October 2026)

We asked Google AI Overviews, Gemini (with Google Search) and Claude (with web search) the same 48 buying questions across six software categories, then recorded which brands each answer named, which it named first and which sources it cited. Here is what 115 answers say about how AI assistants pick brands, and what that means if you sell software.

Key findings

  • YouTube is the most cited source in Google AI Overviews: 33 of 42 AI Overview answers (79%) linked at least one YouTube video. Reddit followed at 45%.
  • Each engine reads different sources. AI Overviews lean on YouTube, Reddit and Quora; Gemini on PCMag, Zapier and CNET; Claude on specialist review sites such as security.org, TechRadar and Cybernews.
  • Zapier's blog shows up everywhere: it was cited in 30 of 115 answers (26%), in five of the six categories.
  • The engines often disagree on the top pick. For project management, AI Overviews named Asana first most often, Claude named ClickUp and Gemini named monday.com. In email marketing each engine had a different favourite.
  • The biggest brand isn't always the default answer. In email marketing, MailerLite (88% of answers) and Brevo (81%) were named more often than Mailchimp (75%). LastPass appeared in only 19% of password-manager answers.
  • Some categories have a clear default: Wix was named first in 15 of 21 website-builder answers, by all three engines, and HubSpot was every engine's most frequent first pick for CRM.
  • Gemini cited the most sources per answer (9.8 on average), AI Overviews 7.5 and Claude 4.0.

What this means if you sell software

  • Get on YouTube. Google AI Overviews cited a YouTube video in about four out of five answers, so a clear review, demo or comparison video is one of the most direct ways in.
  • Earn a place in the lists AI reads. Zapier's 'best apps' posts, PCMag, TechRadar, CNET and category review sites (security.org, tooltester.com, emailtooltester.com) were cited again and again.
  • Be part of the conversation on Reddit and Quora. AI Overviews cited Reddit in close to half of their answers.
  • Track each engine separately. They rely on different sources and often name a different brand first, so a good showing in one says little about the others.
  • Measure the trend, not one snapshot. AI answers change as their sources change, so re-check the same questions monthly.

Method

On 5 October 2026 we ran AutomationNation's AI Visibility Tracker with eight buying questions in each of six software categories (48 prompts, listed below), asking each question once to Google AI Overviews (US Google Search), Gemini with Google Search grounding and Claude with web search. 115 of 144 answers were usable: Google AI Overviews didn't appear for every question and some Gemini requests hit rate limits, which left 42 AI Overview, 48 Claude and 25 Gemini answers. A brand counts as named when its name appears in the answer; 'named first' is the tracked brand that appears earliest. Sources are the distinct domains each answer cites. AI answers vary between runs, locations and users, so treat the percentages as indicative.
