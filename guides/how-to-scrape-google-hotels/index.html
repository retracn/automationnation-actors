How to scrape Google Hotels in 2026 (prices for your dates)

There's no public API for Google Hotels search results. With Google Hotels Scraper on Apify you enter a city or area and your dates, click Start, and get up to 20 hotels per search in seconds with nightly prices, rating, reviews, class, coordinates and photos, for $1 per 1,000 hotels.

Open Google Hotels Scraper on Apify $1 per 1,000 hotels ($0.80 on Gold and above)

Step by step

  1. Create a free Apify account. Sign up at apify.com. The free plan includes $5 of monthly credit, about 5,000 hotels with this Actor, and needs no credit card.
  2. Open Google Hotels Scraper. Go to apify.com/automationnation/google-hotels-scraper and click Try for free.
  3. Enter locations and dates. Add cities, areas or searches like "hotels near Times Square", plus check-in and check-out.
  4. Filter. Minimum rating, minimum stars and maximum price per night.
  5. Monitor rates. Schedule the Actor daily to track prices by date.

Do it from code

Python

# pip install apify-client
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN")
run = client.actor("automationnation/google-hotels-scraper").call(run_input={
  "locations": [
    "Paris"
  ],
  "checkIn": "2026-11-20",
  "checkOut": "2026-11-23"
})
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(item.get("name"), item.get("pricePerNight"), item.get("rating"), item.get("hotelClass"))

REST API (cURL)

curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/automationnation~google-hotels-scraper/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"locations": ["Paris"], "checkIn": "2026-11-20", "checkOut": "2026-11-23"}'
