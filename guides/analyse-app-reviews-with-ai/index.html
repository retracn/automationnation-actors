How to analyse App Store and Google Play reviews with AI

To turn app reviews into a to-do list, scrape the newest reviews from both stores and summarise them with AI. App Store & Google Play Reviews Scraper + AI on Apify does it in one run: give it app names or store links and it returns, per app and store, rating statistics, ratings per version, top bugs, feature requests, critical issues and competitor mentions, for $0.05 per app report.

Step by step

  1. Open the Actor. Go to apify.com/automationnation/app-store-review-miner and click Try for free (about 100 app reports a month on Apify's free credit).
  2. Add apps. Enter app names, App Store / Google Play links or IDs. Add several App Store countries to combine reviews from different markets.
  3. Choose how many reviews. Up to 500 per app per store; the AI analysis reads the newest ones.
  4. Run and read the report. Each report lists top bugs, feature requests, critical issues (crashes, billing, login, data loss) and ratings per app version — the fastest way to spot a bad release.
  5. Monitor weekly. Turn on only-new-reviews mode and schedule it; apps with no new reviews cost nothing.

Do it from code

Python

# pip install apify-client
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN")
run = client.actor("automationnation/app-store-review-miner").call(run_input={
  "appNames": [
    "Duolingo"
  ]
})
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(item.get("appName"), item.get("platform"), item.get("avgRating"), item.get("topBugsList"))
