How to scrape App Store reviews in 2026 (past the 500-review limit)

Apple's public RSS review feed stops at the newest 500 reviews per country, so most tools can't go further. App Store Reviews Scraper on Apify reads the review API behind apps.apple.com instead: give it App Store links, App IDs or app names and the countries you want, click Start, and get one row per review with stars, title, text, date, author and the developer's reply, for $0.08 per 1,000 reviews.

Step by step

  1. Create a free Apify account. Sign up at apify.com. The free plan includes $5 of monthly credit, over 60,000 reviews with this Actor, and needs no credit card.
  2. Open App Store Reviews Scraper. Go to apify.com/automationnation/app-store-reviews-scraper and click Try for free.
  3. Add apps and countries. Paste App Store URLs, numeric App IDs (e.g. 324684580) or app names (e.g. Duolingo), and optionally list storefronts such as us, gb and de.
  4. Set the limit and filters. Choose Max reviews per app, newest or most helpful first, and optionally a star range such as 1 to 2 stars for complaints.
  5. Run and export. Click Start. Download JSON, CSV or Excel, or read the results through the API. The Developer replies view shows answered reviews.
  6. Automate it. Tick Only new reviews and schedule the Actor daily, then send new reviews to Slack, email, Google Sheets or a webhook through Apify integrations.

Do it from code

Python

# pip install apify-client
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN")
run = client.actor("automationnation/app-store-reviews-scraper").call(run_input={
  "apps": [
    "https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spotify-music-and-podcasts/id324684580",
    "570060128"
  ],
  "countries": [
    "us",
    "gb"
  ],
  "maxReviewsPerApp": 1000,
  "sort": "newest"
})
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(item.get("appName"), item.get("rating"), item.get("date"), item.get("title"))
