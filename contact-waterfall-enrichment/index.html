Find contact emails for a list of company websites

Contact Waterfall Enrichment is an Apify Actor that finds the best contact email for each company in a list: published emails from the website first, then UK Companies House director names with common email patterns verified against the domain's mail servers.

Run it on Apify $0.015 per company

Quick facts

  • Scrapes homepages and contact / about / team pages, JSON-LD and obfuscated emails.
  • Falls back to Companies House director names for UK companies and MX-verified email patterns.
  • Every result carries a confidence level (named contact, generic contact, verified pattern, none).
  • Price: $0.015 per company.
